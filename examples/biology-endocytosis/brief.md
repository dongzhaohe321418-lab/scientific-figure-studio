# Biology live test brief

Conceptual mammalian clathrin-mediated endocytosis: cargo capture, pit maturation, scission and uncoating. Intended for a research overview; selected factors only. Original composition, no third-party figure pixels supplied to image generation.

Deliver PNG and fully editable SVG saved in this persistent project directory; preserve an image2 visual master and a reproducible vector generator. Test subsequent offline editing and save/reopen behaviour.

Hard invariants: extracellular cargo remains in the pit/vesicle lumen; receptor orientation is preserved; AP2 and clathrin are on the cytosolic side; dynamin is at the connected pit neck; post-scission vesicle is closed and disconnected; uncoating removes coat, not membrane. Use GTP-dependent scission and ATP-dependent Hsc70/auxilin uncoating. Assembly trajectories vary; four panels are conceptual snapshots, not a universal timing model. No quantitative or atomic-scale claims.

Visual brief: four aligned panels, restrained warm membrane/amber coat/teal receptor/blue adaptor/plum dynamin palette, coherent membrane thickness, subtle gradients, live labels, sparse leaders and clear whitespace. Cross-section and simple protein glyphs support topology over photorealism.
