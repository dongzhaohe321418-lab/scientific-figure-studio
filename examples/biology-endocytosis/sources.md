# Sources and actual visual inspection

Accessed 8 September 2026. This is an original conceptual synthesis, not a reproduction of a published figure.

1. Bucher et al. (2018), Nature Communications, DOI [10.1038/s41467-018-03533-0](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-03533-0). Article introduction, results and Figure 1: coat curvature models and membrane/cytosolic coat relationships. Figure 1 was actually opened as a local image after retrieval from the publisher's linked image URL. Visual observations: repeated membrane sections, sparse labels, stage coding and separated mechanistic/quantitative panels. We use an original four-stage layout and do not copy their data or geometry. The paper's BSC-1-specific trajectory is not asserted as universal.
2. Scott et al. (2018), [Membrane bending occurs at all stages of clathrin-coat assembly and defines endocytic dynamics](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41467-018-02818-8). DOI 10.1038/s41467-018-02818-8. Abstract/introduction read: alternative bending trajectories prevent a universal flat-first claim. Its figures were not used as visually inspected references.
3. Pucadyil and Schmid (2008), Cell, DOI [10.1016/j.cell.2008.11.020](https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/19084268/). PubMed abstract and figure captions read: dynamin assemblies at necks and GTP-supported fission. PMC full-text retrieval presented a browser challenge; no full-text inspection is claimed for this paper. The cartoon does not assert a particular dynamin power-stroke model or nucleotide stoichiometry.
4. Xing et al. (2010; advance online 2009), EMBO Journal, DOI 10.1038/emboj.2009.383. [Author-hosted full text](https://kirchhausen.hms.harvard.edu/sites/kirchhausen.hms.harvard.edu/files/publication-attachments/EMBO_J_2009_Xing.pdf). Introduction and Figure 7 caption read; PDF page 7 rendered and visually inspected. Auxilin recruits Hsc70 for ATP-dependent clathrin disassembly. Visual observations: molecule-specific colours, readable process arrows, separation between a simplified cycle and structural insets. We omit atomic detail and do not copy the figure.

Research-only downloaded reference images/PDF remain outside the public skill repository. Generated examples contain no copied source-image pixels. Protein icons, membrane geometry and particle counts are schematic and do not represent measured stoichiometry.
