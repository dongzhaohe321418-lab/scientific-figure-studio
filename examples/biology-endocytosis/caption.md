# Figure caption

Clathrin-mediated endocytosis in a conceptual mammalian-cell pathway. (a) Cargo is captured by membrane receptors while selected cytosolic adaptor and coat components assemble. (b) A connected, invaginated pit carries a dynamin neck collar; scission is GTP dependent. (c) The detached coated vesicle preserves cargo in its lumen and the cytosolic orientation of adaptor/coat components. (d) Auxilin and Hsc70 support ATP-dependent clathrin coat removal while the vesicle membrane remains intact.

Colours and shapes identify cargo (coral sphere), receptor (teal transmembrane glyph), AP2 (blue adaptor), clathrin (amber scaffold/triskelion) and dynamin (plum collar).
