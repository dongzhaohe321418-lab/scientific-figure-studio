# Figure caption

Three illustrative directed acyclic graphs distinguish confounding, mediation and collider conditioning. Z is a pre-exposure common cause in the first graph; M lies on one causal pathway in the second. A direct X→Y edge remains in both, so blocking the other depicted path does not imply conditional independence of X and Y. In the third graph, X→C←Y is blocked without conditioning on C and can support a noncausal association after conditioning. The dashed outline indicates conditioning, not an extra edge. Graphs encode assumptions; they do not establish empirical effects, complete identification or a universal adjustment rule.
