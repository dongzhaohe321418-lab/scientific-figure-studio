# Figure caption

Nine selected carbon-transfer pathways connect atmospheric CO₂, plant and herbivore biomass, soil organic matter and microbial biomass. Solid arrows distinguish transfers by their source, destination and label; the dashed ochre light arrow denotes energy input separately. Plants, the generic herbivore and microbes are conceptual depictions, not species identification. Microbes are enlarged and soil roots are schematic. The diagram is neither a complete carbon budget nor a steady-state model; no pool sizes, flux rates or measured ecosystem response are implied.
