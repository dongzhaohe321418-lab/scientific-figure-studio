# Figure caption

Hydroxide displaces bromide from methyl bromide in a concerted SN2 substitution. Axial O–C bond formation and C–Br bond breaking occur in one transition state; dashed bonds denote partial bonds, not an intermediate. The lower panel shows the selected attacking lone pair and the departing bond pair, using full arrowheads for electron pairs. Other lone pairs are omitted. All four hydrogens and total formal charge −1 are retained in each state. This methyl substrate is achiral: stereochemical inversion is not tested here. Projection and atom sizes are conceptual; no measured bond lengths, angles, kinetics or energetics are depicted.
