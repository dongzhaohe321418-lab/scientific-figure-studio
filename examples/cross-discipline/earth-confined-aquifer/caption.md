# Figure caption

A conceptual permeable bed receives recharge at an elevated outcrop and becomes confined beneath a low-permeability unit. The cased standpipe is screened only within the saturated aquifer. Its static water surface matches the local head marker and lies above local land but below the pipe rim, so the pictured pipe does not overflow. A separate outlet below available head may discharge; flow changes local head. The dashed line is a local marker, not a measured regional potentiometric surface. Strata, grains and vertical exaggeration are illustrative, without site data or a numerical groundwater model.
